Privacy Policy: Quiet Shift Uploader

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Quiet Shift Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of The Quiet Shift to upload the channel's own videos to the channel's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission (TikTok Login Kit), it reads basic profile info (display name, open ID) to confirm the right account, and uses the video upload permission to send videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts.

What it stores

Only the access tokens TikTok issues, kept on the owner's own computer. It does not collect, sell or share any data about other TikTok users or viewers.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions.
